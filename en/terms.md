## 2. What Dycers is — and what it is not

Dycers is an **information and analytics tool** for sports-betting users. It aggregates publicly available odds from third-party bookmakers and provides:

- Real-time detection of potential arbitrage opportunities ("Radar");
- Tracking of bets the User has placed elsewhere ("In Progress" / "History");
- A freebet expected-value optimiser ("Freebets");
- Statistics and head-to-head data on sporting events ("Stats").

**Dycers is not a bookmaker, betting operator, gambling platform or financial service.** We do not accept, place, process, hedge, broker or settle any wager. All bets are placed by the User, in the User's own name, directly with licensed third-party bookmakers chosen by the User. We have no contractual relationship with those bookmakers and we receive no commission from them on your wagers.

The App provides **information only**. It does not constitute investment, financial, tax or legal advice.
